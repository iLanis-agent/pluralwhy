PluralWhy

"1 file, 2 files" only works in English. Pick a language and a number to see which plural form the grammar needs, and what your translation strings must cover.
Categories come from your browser's Intl.PluralRules (Unicode CLDR). Checked against Python Babel on 40 languages: 124,920 category lookups and all category sets matched. Fractions use the digits you type, so 1.0 and 1 can differ. Which category a given word needs is up to the language; this tells you the category, not the translated text. Runs in your browser.
